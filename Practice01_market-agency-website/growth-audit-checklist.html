The 10-Point Growth Audit Checklist

Work through this in under 15 minutes to find where your marketing funnel is leaking, and what to fix first.

  1. Traffic source mix
    Are you over-reliant on a single channel? List your top three sources and their share of total traffic.
  2. Mobile load time
    Test your top landing page on mobile. Under 3 seconds is the bar — anything slower is losing you leads.
  3. One clear CTA above the fold
    Every core page should have a single, obvious next step — not three competing buttons.
  4. Unique meta titles & descriptions
    Check your top 20 pages. Duplicate or missing tags are a quiet but common ranking leak.
  5. Lead form friction
    Count the fields on your main form. Every field beyond email and name can cost you 5–10% conversion.
  6. Email capture on your top page
    Your highest-traffic page should always have a way to capture an email, even if it isn't the goal of that page.
  7. Consistent UTM tagging
    Spot-check your last five campaigns. Inconsistent tagging quietly breaks your attribution.
  8. CAC vs. LTV
    Do you know both numbers for this quarter? If not, that's the first thing to fix before scaling spend.
  9. Shared reporting cadence
    Does everyone on the team look at the same dashboard, or does each person have their own numbers?
  10. One channel to cut
    Name the one underperforming channel you could pause this month, and what you'd do with the budget instead.
